The prompt

Every model got exactly this, word for word.

Create ONE self-contained index.html with an interactive 3D model of India Gate (New Delhi) using Three.js r160 via importmap from unpkg (use three/addons/ for OrbitControls, Sky, EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, OutputPass). Use only procedural geometry and canvas-generated textures, with no external assets. Output only the complete code in one block, no explanation.

CREATIVE BRIEF (this part is yours):
Capture the real monument's recognisable character: a tall sandstone memorial arch with a through-passage, stepped upper tiers, ornamental frieze, carved roundels, the "INDIA" inscription, and the domed canopy behind it. You choose the proportions, materials, textures, surrounding scenery, atmosphere, colour palette, animated details and any extra touches you think will look best. Make it look like a showpiece you'd be proud to post. Surprise me with the details.

FIXED REQUIREMENTS (do not change these):

Lighting modes, switched smoothly with eased transitions of sun, sky, fog, exposure and bloom:
- Day
- Golden hour
- Night
- Tricolour: night with the gate lit in horizontal bands, saffron at the top, white in the middle, green at the bottom. Interpret the look as you like.

UI (HTML overlay, translucent glass-style panels, system font):
- Top-left title card reading "INDIA GATE" with a small saffron/white/green bar.
- Top-right hint card: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, keys 1-4, H hides UI.
- Bottom-centre bar with two button groups:
  1) Lighting: Day | Golden hour | Night | Tricolour (active one highlighted).
  2) Camera: Front | Corner | Look up | Through arch | Rear | Aerial | Orbit toggle. Each camera button tweens the camera position and orbit target over about 1.8 seconds with cubic easing.
- Keyboard: keys 1-4 switch lighting, R toggles auto-orbit, H hides all UI.

Controls and rendering:
- OrbitControls with damping and a slow auto-rotate on load. Dragging the scene stops auto-orbit. Limit zoom distance and keep the camera above the ground.
- Soft shadows, ACES tone mapping, bloom post-processing, fog.
- Handle window resize, and use lighter settings on small screens.

QUALITY BAR: complete, no TODOs or placeholders, no console errors, and it runs when the file is opened directly in a browser.

The follow-up. Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 left out the Amar Jawan Jyoti, so both got this same follow-up, word for word.

Here is the India Gate scene you built earlier (attached). Add the Amar Jawan Jyoti under the central arch:

- a black marble cenotaph on a stepped plinth, centred in the arch passage, with "AMAR JAWAN" inscribed in gold on its sides
- an L1A1 rifle standing upright with the barrel pointing down, and a soldier's helmet resting on its butt
- four urns, one at each corner of the plinth, each with a burning flame

The flames should flicker and give off a warm light that falls on the cenotaph and the underside of the arch. Keep the glow subtle in daylight and stronger at night. It should look right in every existing lighting mode.

Keep the scale true to the real monument relative to the arch. Do not change anything else in the scene: geometry, lighting modes, camera presets, controls and performance should all stay as they are. Return the complete updated single HTML file.
